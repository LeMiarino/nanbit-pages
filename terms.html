The free version, and that it stays free

Nanbit is free to download and free to use. The free version is a permanent tier rather than a trial period: it does not run out, it does not become read-only, and there is no point at which you must pay to go on using what you have already put in.

Free use includes the whole of the daily loop — adding up to five habits, marking them done, streaks, the Today screen, the daily reminder and one reminder time for each habit, and the calendar of what you have done: every past day and month to look at, and today and the week before it to change.

Nanbit Pro

Nanbit Pro is an optional subscription. It removes the free version's limits and adds color themes and more reminders — there is no separate Pro app. It unlocks:

  • Unlimited habits, instead of five. A habit you delete no longer counts toward the five, and its past days stay.
  • Color themes — the app in Jade, Teal, Sky, Iris, Plum, Rose, Brick, Cocoa, Honey, Olive or Fern instead of orange. If a subscription ends, the app goes back to orange.
  • More reminders — up to three times a day for each habit instead of one, and a nudge: one more reminder at a time you choose, which comes only if the habit is still not done.
  • Your full history — changing any past day, where the free version changes today and the week before it; the Year view on History; and on Progress, each habit's earlier months and its Year view.

Nothing you have already made is ever taken away. If a subscription lapses, every habit and every completion stays, every habit can still be edited, and every day of it can still be looked at on History. What the free limits control is what you can add or change from then on (another habit, a day more than a week back, a reminder time after the first, and the views and colors Nanbit Pro unlocks), never what is already there. A lapsed subscriber holding more than five habits keeps all of them. Each habit's first reminder keeps going; its other reminder times and its nudge stop, and are kept for if you subscribe again.

Payment, and who takes it

Nanbit Pro is sold as a monthly or an annual subscription. The price you see in the app is the price Google Play reports for your country and currency at that moment; it is not stored in the app, so what is on the screen is what the store will charge.

  • Google Play processes the payment. Nanbit does not take payments and never receives your card or bank details.
  • Payment is charged to your Google Play account when you confirm the purchase — or, if you started a free trial, when that trial ends.
  • A subscription renews automatically at the end of each period unless you cancel before that period ends. Google Play takes the next payment when the new period starts, so canceling any time before then is enough.
  • Your subscription is attached to your Google Play account, because there is no Nanbit account. That is what lets You → See plans → Restore work on a reinstall or a new device without signing into anything.

Free trials, where they are offered

A free trial is sometimes offered on a plan. Whether you can have one is Google Play's decision, not ours — an account that has used the trial before is shown the same plan without it, and in that case the purchase is charged straight away. Nanbit only advertises a trial when the store confirms you are eligible for it.

If you do start a trial, it is free for its stated length and then converts into a paid subscription at the normal price unless you cancel before it ends. Canceling during the trial means you are not charged when it ends: Nanbit Pro stays on until the trial ends, and then you are back on the free version with everything you tracked intact.

Canceling

To cancel, go to subscriptions in Google Play, select the subscription and tap Cancel subscription. The subscription lives with the store rather than with us, so we cannot cancel it on your behalf.

Canceling stops the renewal. It does not delete anything: your habits, your completions and your history are on your phone and are untouched by the state of a subscription.

Refunds

Refunds for a Play purchase are handled by Google Play under Google's own refund policy, through your Play account or Google's support. We have no way to issue a refund for a purchase we never processed. If something has gone wrong, write to us anyway — see below — and we will help where we can.

Your habits, and your backups

Nanbit stores your habits, completions and settings on your device, in the app's own storage. There is no account, no server copy and nothing for us to restore from. Everything that is stored, and the few things that leave the phone, is in the privacy policy.

The practical consequence is worth stating plainly: keeping a copy is up to you. If a phone is lost, reset, or has Nanbit removed, we cannot recover what was on it. The phone's own Google backup may hold a copy — expected, not yet confirmed; the privacy policy says what is known — but that copy is between you and Google, and we cannot reach it. Nanbit has no other way to make a copy.
